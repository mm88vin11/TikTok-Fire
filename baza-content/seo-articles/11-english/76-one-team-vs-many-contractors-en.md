# One studio or many contractors

The usual setup: website from an agency, ads from a freelancer, CRM from an integrator, bot from someone else. Each part works. Leads still disappear:

- the form emails a lead nobody reads;
- the bot doesn't know the client wrote on the site;
- the ad specialist sees "leads", not payments;
- when something breaks, everyone says "my part works".

| | One team (BAZA) | Several contractors |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the gaps | One team | Nobody |
| Revenue per ad visible | Yes, tracking before launch | Rarely |
| Speed of changes | One chat | 3–4 people to align |
