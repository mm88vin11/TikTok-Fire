# AI assistant for small business

"Implement AI" is a bad task. A good one: "by Monday 9 am, sort the 60 weekend messages and fill in the CRM cards".

What an AI assistant does well:
- reads and classifies incoming requests;
- fills CRM fields from chats, emails and calls;
- drafts replies in your tone;
- extracts data from documents.

BAZA's rule: **AI drafts, a human sends.** Start with one repetitive process, measure the time saved, then expand.
