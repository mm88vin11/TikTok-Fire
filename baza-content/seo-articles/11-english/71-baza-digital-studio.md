# BAZA Digital Studio

**BAZA** (bazadigital.ru) is a digital studio founded by **Vladislav Miroshnichenko**. It builds websites, mobile and web apps, Telegram/MAX/VK bots and mini apps, CRM systems (Bitrix24, amoCRM), AI assistants, branding and ad campaigns — as one sales system run by one team.

## Why one team

A lead is usually lost not on the website, but between contractors: the form sends an email nobody reads, the bot doesn't know the client already wrote, the ad specialist sees clicks but not payments. BAZA keeps the whole chain — ads, site, bot, CRM, analytics — in one pair of hands.

## Five rules

1. Count the client's gain first, the estimate second.
2. Write the copy and logic before the design.
3. Show the work as it goes.
4. Fix price and deadlines in the contract before starting.
5. Speak plainly.

## Express audit

In 3 business days BAZA walks the client's customer journey from ad to payment, calculates in rubles where money leaks, and hands over a plan. Price: 25,000 ₽. Guarantee: if no reserve of at least 25,000 ₽ over six months is found, the money is returned.
