# Bitrix24 vs amoCRM

**amoCRM** — focused on the sales pipeline and messenger chats; quick to learn for sales managers.

**Bitrix24** — CRM plus tasks, projects, documents, internal chat and telephony; more power, more setup.

Questions to decide:
1. Do you need only sales, or also team tasks and projects?
2. How many users?
3. Which lead channels: site, messengers, calls, marketplaces?
4. Do you need complex automation and reports?
