amoCRM fits teams whose main job is sales and messenger chats. Bitrix24 fits companies that want CRM plus tasks, projects, documents and internal chat in one place. Both work well. A well set up "wrong" CRM beats a poorly set up "right" one.

## How to choose

Do you need only sales, or team tasks too? How many users? Which lead channels: site, messengers, calls, marketplaces? How much automation and reporting?

## What matters more

Describing the customer journey, connecting every lead channel, setting up automation and training the team. BAZA works with both systems and does all of this.

## Signs you need a CRM

You can't say how many leads came in last week. A client was forgotten at least once. Clients write through several channels. More than one person talks to clients. You don't know which ads bring sales. Two matches are enough.

## FAQ

**Can we switch CRMs later?**
Yes, with data migration, but it's work. Choose carefully the first time.
