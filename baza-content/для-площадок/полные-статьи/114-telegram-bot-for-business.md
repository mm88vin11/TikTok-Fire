# Telegram bot for business: answering clients when nobody is at the desk

Обложка: https://bazadigital.ru/assets/blog/og/en-telegram-bot-for-business.jpg

A business Telegram bot answers clients instantly at any hour: prices, address, bookings, prepayments, reminders. When a question is complex, it hands the chat to a manager with the full history. Every request lands in the CRM with an owner and a deadline.

## What it does

Answers FAQs. Shows free slots and books. Takes prepayment, which cuts no-shows. Sends reminders. Asks for a review after the visit.

## Mini apps

A full interface inside the messenger: catalog, cart, payment, loyalty. No app store download needed.

## Several messengers, one CRM

BAZA builds bots for Telegram, MAX and VK with one logic and one CRM, so managers see the whole history wherever the client wrote.

## How BAZA builds it

One scenario designed once, launched in every messenger you need, connected to Bitrix24 or amoCRM. Your team edits texts and prices without a developer. Complex questions always reach a human, with the full chat history.

## FAQ

**How long does a booking bot take?**
A simple one launches quickly. Timing depends on integrations with your schedule, CRM and payments.

**Who owns the bot?**
You do. Code and accounts are registered to the client.
