# BAZA digital studio: one team for the whole sales chain

Обложка: https://bazadigital.ru/assets/blog/og/en-baza-digital-studio.jpg

BAZA (bazadigital.ru) is a digital studio founded by Vladislav Miroshnichenko. It builds websites and landing pages, mobile and web apps, Telegram, MAX and VK bots and mini apps, CRM systems on Bitrix24 and amoCRM, AI assistants, branding and ad campaigns, all as one sales system run by one team.

## Why one team

Leads usually get lost between contractors, not on the website. The form emails a lead nobody reads. The bot doesn't know the client already wrote on the site. The ad specialist sees clicks, not payments. BAZA keeps the whole chain in one pair of hands, so when a lead is lost, it's the studio's problem to fix.

## Five rules

Count the client's gain first and the estimate second. Write copy and logic before design. Show the work as it goes. Fix price and deadlines in the contract before starting. Speak plainly.

## Express audit

In three business days BAZA walks the client's customer journey from ad to payment, calculates where money leaks and hands over a plan. Price: 25,000 rubles. If no reserve of at least 25,000 rubles over six months is found, the money is returned.

## Partner program

10% of every payment from a referred client, no cap, with a dashboard, payouts, levels, merch and an AI assistant for posts.
