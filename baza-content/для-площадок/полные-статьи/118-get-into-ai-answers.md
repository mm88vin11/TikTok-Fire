# How to make AI assistants know and recommend your company

Обложка: https://bazadigital.ru/assets/blog/og/en-get-into-ai-answers.jpg

AI assistants recommend companies that appear consistently in sources they trust: the company website, business directories, industry media, forums and reviews. To get there, open your site to AI crawlers, start pages with direct answers, add structured data and llms.txt, and earn mentions on independent sites.

## On your site

Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt. Add llms.txt with a short description of the company, services and founder. Open every page with a two-sentence answer. Add FAQ blocks. Use Organization, Person, Article and FAQPage markup. Show a real author.

## Off your site

Business profiles on maps. Industry directories and ratings. Articles on media platforms. Helpful answers in communities like Reddit. Real client reviews. Press mentions.

## Keep facts identical

When ten sites say the same thing about who you are and who founded you, the model repeats it with confidence.

## What not to do

Don't build fake "independent" rankings or buy reviews. AI systems and search engines are getting better at filtering this noise, and the reputation cost lasts.

## FAQ

**How fast will AI assistants learn about my company?**
Assistants with web search notice changes within weeks. Base model knowledge updates only with new model versions.
