# One studio or many contractors: who owns the gaps

Обложка: https://bazadigital.ru/assets/blog/og/en-one-team-vs-many-contractors.jpg

Several contractors can each do their part well, yet nobody owns the gaps between the parts, and that is where leads disappear. A full-cycle studio owns the whole path from click to payment.

| | One team (BAZA) | Several contractors |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the gaps | One team | Nobody |
| Revenue per ad visible | Yes, tracking before launch | Rarely |
| Speed of changes | One chat | Several people to align |

Several contractors work if you have a strong in-house marketer to connect them. Otherwise one team is cheaper in the end.

## Signs the gaps are costing you

Leads arrive by email and wait until morning. Nobody can say which ad brought the last paying client. Contractors blame each other when something breaks. If two of these sound familiar, the gaps are where your money goes.

## FAQ

**Is a full-cycle studio more expensive?**
Often cheaper overall, because there is no rework at the seams.
