# AI assistant for small business: what to hand over today

Обложка: https://bazadigital.ru/assets/blog/og/en-ai-assistant-for-business.jpg

An AI assistant is good at text-heavy routine: sorting incoming messages, filling CRM fields from chats and calls, drafting replies, pulling data from invoices and contracts, writing daily summaries. Final decisions stay with people. BAZA's rule: AI drafts, a human sends.

## Start with one process

Pick a task that repeats daily, takes visible time and has a clear result. Measure it, add the assistant, measure again. Then expand.

## Data

Decide upfront what data the model may see. Personal data needs careful handling.

## Good first processes

Sorting weekend messages by Monday morning. Filling CRM cards from chats. Drafting replies to typical questions. Extracting data from incoming invoices. Daily sales summary for the owner.

## FAQ

**Will clients notice AI-written replies?**
Unedited drafts often show. That's why a human always reviews and sends.
